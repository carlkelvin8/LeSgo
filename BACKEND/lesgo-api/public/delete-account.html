Delete your LeSgo account

This page is the official account-deletion resource for the LeSgo app (com.lesgo_customer).

Request account deletion

You can request permanent deletion even if you no longer have the app installed.

Email an account-deletion request

Send the request to lesgoclaveria@gmail.com and include the email address or phone number registered to your LeSgo account. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Never send your password or one-time password.

Email requests are completed as soon as reasonably possible and within 30 days.

Delete directly in the app

  1. Open LeSgo and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Under Account, select Delete account permanently.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

Before deleting, complete or cancel active orders and withdraw or use any remaining LesPay wallet balance.

Data deleted or anonymized

  • Your account credentials, name, email address, phone number, profile photo, birth date, and saved addresses
  • Customer or driver profile details, uploaded verification documents, and driver location history
  • Chat messages, support requests, reviews, social-sharing records, notifications, sessions, and authentication tokens linked to the account

Your account is disabled permanently and cannot be recovered after deletion.

Data that may be retained

Completed order, transaction, payment, fraud-prevention, security, and audit records may be retained in restricted or anonymized form only for legitimate legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, and security obligations. They are no longer available through the deleted account and are removed or anonymized when the applicable retention obligation ends.
