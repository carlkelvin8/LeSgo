2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
LeSgo is an on-demand delivery and booking platform in the Philippines. Customers use the separate LeSgo app to order food (LesEat) and goods (LesBuy). LeSgo currently offers goods and food delivery only; we do not carry passengers. LeSgo Affiliates is the partner app for the people who fulfil those orders:
- Riders: register, verify identity and vehicle, go online, accept jobs, navigate, chat with the customer about the active order, and track earnings.
- Merchants (restaurants and stores): receive and manage orders, edit their menu, print receipts and view sales.
Anyone in our service area can apply as a rider or merchant; the app is not limited to one company's employees. It gives local merchants and independent riders a way to earn.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Laravel Cloud: backend API and database
- Laravel Reverb: real-time order and chat updates
- Firebase Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics: push notifications and crash reports
- Google Maps Platform: maps, geocoding and routing; optional hand-off to Google Maps or Waze
- PayMongo and Xendit (GCash, Maya, cards): registration fees, rider packages and wallet top-ups. These are for real-world delivery services and platform access, not digital content.
- Semaphore and Twilio: SMS one-time passwords
- Google Gemini API: AI assistant in the in-app support chat
- Bluetooth thermal printers (optional receipts)
